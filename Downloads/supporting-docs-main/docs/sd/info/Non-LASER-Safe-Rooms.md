# Non-LASER Safe Rooms (and other secure lab access)

## LTHT Safe Rooms 

LIDA has a number of safe rooms dedicated to directly accessing LTHT systems. These can be booked through lida@leeds.ac.uk, but they are required to log usage and collect evidence that access can be granted to the rooms. 

The process for the Office Management Team to allow bookings in the safe rooms as follows:  
- person must sent the request to us from their nhs.net email address
- email must include confirmation of their LTHT Honorary Contract
- email must also have something from LTHT approving the project and access to their data via the LTHT safer rooms in LIDA.

If that can be sent to [lida@leeds.ac.uk](lida@leeds.ac.uk) Kim and Karen can pick it up.

They will confirm back to them and arrange access etc. once they have this and advise them how to book. They keep a log of those with access and they inform Geoff and Johnny Chagger at the Trust each time access is given to someone.

## ONS Secure Research Service (SRS)

The ONS make use of the UKDS' infrastructure to make their data available from within a secure environment and often link to pages describing the UKDS Secure Labs. They do have different processes for approving access to their secure environments so for all practical purposes they should be treated as distinct entities.
