# LTHT Transfer Mechanisms

In LIDA we work with LTHT to transfer, store and process data securely either:  
- on our LASER platform  
OR 
- from [LIDA's LTHT SafeRooms](Non-LASER-Safe-Rooms.md)  

Once approved by LTHT the data follows one of the two processes below. This has been agreed with LTHT (Geoff Hall, John Corkett and Johnny Chagger).  

## LTHT authorisation for researchers to access LIDA LTHT data via PC saferooms

Researcher sends LIDA: 

- Project approval document, including confirmation that LTHT approves the researcher to access LTHT computer to access LTHT data
- Confirmation of LTHT honorary contract
- Confirmation of NHS email account (if not already received)

## LTHT approval of data extraction from LTHT and transfer to UoL storage

- The researcher will contact LTHT Research and Informatics Team (John) to request data and follow LTHT’s own research data access process. 
- Once the data is approved for release by LTHT, it will be transferred to UoL as per the terms of LTHT’s **project specific** Data Sharing Agreement.  

### Tier 3-4
Is required to be held in LIDA’s LASER platform and will be transferred by BISCOM.  

The process steps required prior to this by the researcher are detailed in LIDA’s Research Management Process.  

### Tier 0-2
Can be stored in other (non-LASER) University storage, subject to LTHT requirements in the **project specific** Data Sharing Agreement.  

The software for transferring the data securely from LTHT to a UoL (non-LASER) endpoint is to be confirmed.
