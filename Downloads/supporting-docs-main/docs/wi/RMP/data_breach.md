# Data Breaches  

All staff and researchers must report any actual or suspected event to DAT in the first instance regardless of how minor they believe it to be.

## Actions  

```
Immediately upon learning of a suspected or actual information security incident:  
- inform the LIDA IG Manager (cc DAT Manager)   
```

The LIDA IG Manager will log all ISMS related incidents in the ISMS incident log and create an incident in Service Now, assigning it to IT Governance Risk & Compliance Team.  

Following an initial assessment by the DAT and LIDA Information Governance Manager, the incident response is as follows:  
1. The IT Governance Risk & Compliance Team carries out an assessment following the UoL policy and procedures.
2. If it is considered that a breach is of such severity that it may have to be reported externally (e.g. clients, Health Research Authority or the Information Commissioners Office), the UoL Secretariat and Data Protection Officer will make an evaluation and agree any additional mitigation and other actions required and the time frame in which these will happen. The University Secretary or Deputy Secretary will, on a case-by-case basis, determine who should report to the relevant authorities.
