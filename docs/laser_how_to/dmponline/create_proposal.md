# Create a LASER Project Proposal in DMPonline

**1. Create a new plan**: Once signed in, click **Create plan** at the top right of the page.
Enter your project title, then under **Your Organisation's Templates**, select **University of Leeds: LASER Project Proposal**.

![Create a new plan page showing the project title field and the LASER Project Proposal template selected](../../images/DMPonline/plan_create.png)

Scroll down and click **Create** at the bottom of the page.

**2. Start writing your plan**: Go to the **Project details** tab and add some general information about your project.

Then head to the **Write Plan** tab and provide more detailed information about your project proposal.

**3. Share your plan with the team**: Once your project proposal is complete, go to the **Share** tab. Under **Invite collaborators**, enter:

```
dat@leeds.ac.uk
```

Set the permission to **Editor**, then click **Submit**. This makes your plan visible to the Data Analytics Team.

![Share tab showing the invite collaborators form with dat@leeds.ac.uk entered and Editor permission selected](../../images/DMPonline/share_page.png)

**4. Notify the team**: Once you've submitted, please email **dat@leeds.ac.uk** to let us know your proposal is complete, so we can review it.
